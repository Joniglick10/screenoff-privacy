Privacy Policy for ScreenOff

Last updated: July 8, 2026

ScreenOff ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

Usage Access (App Usage Data)

ScreenOff uses Android's Usage Access permission (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) to read how much time you spend on apps on your device. This is the core function of the app: measuring your screen time so you can track it, reduce it, and compare it with friends. This data is used only to provide the app's features.

Account Information

When you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information such as your name and email address, used to create your account and identify you on your friends' leaderboard.

Screen Time and Activity Data

Your daily screen time totals, streaks, points, and per-app usage summaries are stored so the app can show your stats and your position on the leaderboard. App usage details used for your on-screen breakdown are processed on your device.

How We Use Your Information

  • To measure and display your screen time.
  • To calculate streaks, points, and leaderboard rankings.
  • To let you compete with friends you choose to connect with.
  • To improve the app's features and reliability.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your name and screen time totals are visible only to people in leagues you join. We do not share your data with advertisers.

Data Storage and Security

Account and stats data is stored securely using Google Firebase. We take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Children's Privacy

ScreenOff is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your Choices

You can stop all data collection at any time by revoking Usage Access in your device settings and uninstalling the app.

Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your ScreenOff account at any time by sending an email to joniglick10@gmail.com with the subject "Delete Account".

When your request is processed:

  • Your ScreenOff account will be permanently deleted.
  • Your profile information will be deleted.
  • Your friends, leagues, leaderboard entries, streaks, points, and stored statistics will be deleted.
  • Any remaining information will only be retained if required by applicable law.

Account deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days.
